## How to use this catalog

1. A new LAD `body` may only use Part names listed above until another name is proven
   by `compile_block` on a real import.
2. `NameCon` in a `Wire` names the pin; `UId` points at the Part. Only these pin names
   appeared in the sample — other pins may exist but are unverified.
3. A negated contact appears as its own `NContact` Part in this export; a hand-built
   `Contact` + `<Negated Name="operand"/>` is a second form (seen in project block
   `ATUO_1`). Both must be verified per CPU family before being relied on.
4. This catalog says nothing about CPU availability: S7-300/400 vs 1200 vs 1500 can
   differ. Treat `compile_block` as the only authority.
